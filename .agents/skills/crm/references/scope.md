# Scope

A minimal CRM manages customers, their conversations and messages. WhatsApp is an external communication channel, not the owner of customers or history. Customers can exist without WhatsApp or a conversation. Stored customers and history remain accessible during channel outages.

Do not add follow-ups, incidents, campaigns, automations, funnels or opportunities without an explicit change in scope. Scheduling is a separate domain: appointments reference existing CRM customers, and manual and WhatsApp-originated bookings share its rules. Customer identity, contact and communication history are distinct.
