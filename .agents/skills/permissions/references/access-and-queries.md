# Identity, Membership, and Permissions

## Access within identity and groups

Cynthia's permission references cover invitations, members, groups, and group permissions. They do not define employees, assignments, certificates, or catalog resources. Category and service structure, including drag-and-drop order, belongs to the catalog skill.

### Users and groups

Manage Users permits creating and deleting invitations and deleting active members, subject to applicable hierarchical restrictions. Manage Permissions and Groups permits managing groups, their permissions, and memberships, subject to hierarchy, permission-editing limits, and the prohibition on directly modifying one's own memberships. Neither permission automatically grants the other; the Owner and users with Administrator have both.

## Group queries

The group list is presented in priority order. Groups can be retrieved in portions so the application can load more results as needed, without requiring users to navigate traditional pagination controls.

If the hierarchy changes while a person is viewing successive portions of the list, the view may need to refresh to reflect the current order. The Owner does not appear in this list as a group.
